Featured Automation Project

AI-Powered Customer Support
Triage & Discord Alerts

An event-driven workflow built to automate support ticket triage using modern workflow orchestration and AI intelligence.

🎯 The Problem

In fast-paced corporate environments and digital businesses, customer support queues are constantly flooded with emails. Manually monitoring the inbox to filter out critical complaints, security risks, or urgent issues leads to significant delays, human errors, and slower response times for high-priority clients.

💡 The Solution

To eliminate manual overhead and ensure zero lag for critical issues, I engineered an automated, event-driven system using n8n, LLMs, and Discord Webhooks. The system reads incoming emails in real-time, leverages AI to evaluate urgency and risk, and instantly routes high-priority alerts to the operations channel.

⚙️ How the Workflow Operates (Architecture)

The workflow functions through a seamless multi-step automated pipeline:

  • 1. Email Trigger (Gmail Node): Automatically captures incoming customer support emails in real-time through secure triggers.
  • 2. AI Analysis & Triage (LLM Node): Passes the email body and subject to an AI model to evaluate sentiment, urgency, and potential risk levels.
  • 3. Conditional Logic (If Node): Evaluates the AI's response to filter out noise and separate routine queries from critical alerts.
  • 4. Real-Time Routing (Discord Webhook): If an email is flagged as high-risk or urgent, an instant alert with a summary is pushed directly to a dedicated Discord support channel.

🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools

  • Workflow Engine: n8n (Containerized via Docker for stable, independent execution)
  • AI / LLM Integration: Advanced Prompt Engineering & AI Model Node for Intent Triage
  • Alert & Messaging: Discord Webhooks
  • Deployment: Docker & Docker Compose

🚀 Business Impact

This automation significantly cuts down manual inbox-monitoring time, ensures that critical customer issues are addressed instantly without human lag, and demonstrates how modern AI orchestration can be plugged directly into enterprise communication tools.
