The Problem

The team needed a way to identify competitor-related words and validate documents before they were reviewed manually.

Manual checking was time-consuming and there was a risk that unwanted competitor references could remain inside documents.

The Solution

I built an automation workflow using n8n, hosted inside a Docker environment, and connected it with Google Sheets and an LLM.

The workflow automatically processed the provided content, identified competitor-related words and supported their removal or validation as part of the document compliance process.

Automation Workflow

  1. Document/content received for validation.
  2. n8n workflow processes the input.
  3. Google Sheets is used as part of the data workflow.
  4. LLM/Gemini analyses the content.
  5. Competitor-related words are identified.
  6. Identified content is processed for removal or compliance validation.
  7. The final result can be reviewed before the document proceeds further.
